Title: CISA AA26-097A - Iranian-Affiliated Cyber Actors Exploit Programmable Logic Controllers Across U.S. Critical Infrastructure
Date: 2026-04-07
Actor: CyberAv3ngers / Iranian-affiliated actors

Summary:
Iranian-affiliated cyber actors targeted internet-facing programmable logic controllers (PLCs) and related operational technology environments across U.S. critical infrastructure.
The detection engineering goal is to start with high-confidence indicators, then promote the detection into behavior that looks for suspicious external access to PLC networks, OT ports, and engineering workstations.
